Welcome to the audio tour of the Assadaka Alraqia showroom in Tripoli. Eight stops, each with commentary. Mute the sound or switch language at any time.

Stop one, the entrance: vessel basins and mirrors sit on lit plinths, so you can see every finish and size as you walk in. Look left and right to get your bearings.

Stop two, the marble corner: a dark marble wall, wall-hung basins, framed mirrors and a glass shower enclosure, so you can picture each piece in your own bathroom.

Stop three, taps and accessories: display stands bring together basins in several models and sizes, alongside mixers and accessories, so comparing pieces up close is easy.

Stop four, the window front: a street view and natural light reveal the true colours of the ceramics and taps, and give a second angle on the shower and basin displays.

Stop five, the CeraStyle corner: the brand display, model shelving and WC units. Zoom in to read the dimensions and compare shapes.

Stop six, reception: a marble-fronted desk, the brand wall and the entrance to the inner departments. From here you can book a visit or ask about any piece on WhatsApp.

Stop seven, the sanitaryware gallery: a wide range of WC units and basins on CeraStyle plinths, with boards describing each model so you can choose what suits your space.

Stop eight, baths: complete bathroom settings in marble and wood finishes, with shower enclosures and WCs. Our Tripoli team can help you choose and confirm availability. Thank you for visiting.
